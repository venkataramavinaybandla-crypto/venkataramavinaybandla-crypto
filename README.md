## Research Work

> These are research-oriented concept papers exploring novel AI architectures, self-evolving systems, and robotics. They represent **proposed frameworks and theoretical/experimental exploration** — not commercially deployed systems.

<details open>
<summary><b>DAS-HDPP — Dimensional Anchor System / Hyper-Dimensional Photonic Processor</b></summary>
<br>

A theoretical/proposed AI architecture exploring a **sparse 4D relational manifold** for representing relationships between concepts — spanning dimensions of syntax, semantics, discourse/context, and relation type.

**Core concepts explored:**
- Causal curvature ("causal gravity") between conceptual anchors
- Wormhole Routing across the relational lattice
- Conformal prediction for uncertainty-aware inference
- Persistent homology for structural analysis of the manifold

**HDPP** is presented as a future-oriented **photonic/hardware** concept intended to eventually support this architecture.

*Status: proposed/theoretical architecture — not a validated or deployed replacement for existing AI systems.*

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Self-Evolving AI in Wireless Systems</b></summary>
<br>

A research paper proposing a lifecycle and architecture for **self-evolving agentic AI** — systems that improve through tool intelligence, workflow optimization, self-reflection, contextual adaptation, and evolutionary learning.

**Framework:** a cooperative **multi-agent system** where role-specialized LLM agents are coordinated by a supervisor agent using structured dialogue, feedback, and validation.

**Case study:** applies the framework to wireless network optimization — evolving from fixed to movable antenna configurations in **LAWNs (Large-scale Antenna Wireless Networks)**.

> Reported **up to 52.02% performance recovery** in this specific case-study setting, with minimal human involvement.

*This figure is a case-study result within the paper's proposed framework — not a claim of general or universal self-evolving AI capability.*

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Dual Hound — Dual-Form Robotic System for Environmental Intelligence</b></summary>
<br>

A proposed dual-form robotic system concept focused on **environmental intelligence, safety, and hazardous-environment monitoring**, built on a shared **Master-Cell / modular architecture** — one underlying intelligence core supporting two physical embodiments.

| Form | Description | Applications |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro-Hound** | Small, bug-like form for confined/hazardous spaces | Gas & chemical detection, environmental sensing, mapping, reconnaissance |
| **Macro-Hound** | Larger, dog-like form for broader industrial use | Gas-leak detection, anomaly monitoring, security monitoring, hazardous-environment assessment |

*Status: research/system concept — no physical prototype or field-testing claims.*

</details>
